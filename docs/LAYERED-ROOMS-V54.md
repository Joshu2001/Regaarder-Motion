# V54 — independent mother and son room layers

The mother’s workshop previously used one flat image. It now has four independently replaceable sources: clean warm room, seated woman, foreground wash tub, and windowsill cloth. The son’s existing independent cutouts are retained, with the cloth moved left and up onto the sill. The mother is shifted left from the former framing. These are generated reference-informed illustrations; exact original layer geometry and camera calibration are not recovered.

## Simple editing

Open **Next chapter → Mother’s room · depth and camera** or **Portrait room · depth and camera**. Use Replace Person / Clean room / Foreground vessel / Cloth to replace one asset. The existing versioned asset store retains replacements and originals. Select a preview action (camera focus, person, vessel, sill cloth) and click the starting preview to place it. Placement clicks set the asset canvas’s upper-left corner; transparent padding remains part of that canvas. Advanced numeric fields give precise X/Y/width, depth, blur and lighting. Camera speed, start and finish are independent. Start/finish are fractions of the shot (0–1); speed1.5 means1.5×. Common animation-library playback controls also allow reverse, delay, pauses and slow/fast timing.

The mother and son have separate settings namespaces (`historyMother*` and `historyRoom*`), so changing one does not change the other. Original-image toggles restore the old flat artwork. The later color portrait remains unchanged by default.
