# V127 sourcing and classifications

## Retrieved vector originals

Unmodified downloads are preserved as adidas-downloaded-original.svg and puma-downloaded-original.svg. Files named *-source.svg are normalized renderer sources; SVG geometry is retained, while fill/viewBox/dimensions were adapted for the runtime.

- Adidas trefoil: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adidas_trefoil_logo.svg ; original https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/Adidas_trefoil_logo.svg . Bundled `adidas-source.svg` and alpha derivative `adidas-vector.png`. Exact trefoil geometry; no generative logo approximation. This is the reference's retrospective trefoil treatment, not a claim that the trefoil existed in 1970.
- PUMA lockup: https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/303336/puma-logo ; download https://www.svgrepo.com/download/303336/puma-logo.svg . Bundled `puma-source.svg` and `puma-vector.png`. White background removed and website footer excluded from vector viewBox; cat, lettering and registered mark retained. Community-hosted reproduction, not claimed to be an official PUMA master.

## Retrieved primary-publisher period scans

PLACAR publisher article: https://placar.com.br/colunas/ha-55-anos-a-receita-de-pele-para-o-tri-na-edicao-numero-1-de-placar/ . Publication identifies the reproduced pages as issue no.1, 20 March 1970; reportage Michel Laurence, photos Lemyr Martins and Agência JB.

- `placar-page-2.jpg`: https://placar.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Vamos_Ganhar_So_Nos_Falta_Humildade.jpg.jpg . Original full spread preserved. First animated sheet displays left page.
- `placar-page-1.jpg`: https://placar.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Captura-de-tela-2025-03-19-225646.jpg . Original page38 scan preserved.

Exact reference newspaper originals were not recovered. A reverse-search upload facility was unavailable; descriptive search located period alternatives. Digitized football publications at the National Library and university repositories were located but direct PDF retrieval returned 403/503. Publisher scans were successfully retrieved instead. These are authentic archival magazine pages, not fabricated newspaper evidence. Source metadata and original dimensions/hashes are in `pele-manifest.json`; generated assets never receive an archival tag.

## Generated assets

Built-in image generation produced the following project assets. They are reconstructed film assets, not historical records:

- `foreground-guests-v127.png`: two independently bound seated near guests; transparent atlas with separate left/right crop rectangles.
- `rival-wall-v127.png`: photographic cyan/red wall plate, no baked logos or people.
- `docs/corrected-money-art-direction.png`: edit of the exact user-selected image `image-edit-target-22ee637b626ae422.png`; supporting reference only, never used as a flattened runtime scene.

Prompt intent: preserve the selected men's identities and left-blue/right-red staging; remove annotations; show three Franklin $100 bills, cinematic flame light and larger foreground bundles. For the atlas: two separate 1960s seated guests in chairs, rear three-quarter angles facing inward, blue foreground fill, transparent background and no text. For the wall: frontal worn plaster, saturated cyan left/crimson right, diagonal volumetric light, realistic film texture, no objects, logos or people.

All V126 sourced currency and photographic originals remain preserved. Replacement images retain effects and are tagged USER IMAGE / source unverified until provenance is supplied.
