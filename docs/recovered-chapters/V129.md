# Regaarder Motion Atelier V129 — 21:25–23:25

This additive patch continues V128 with the closing memorial fade and a reconstruction of the creator-promo effects in The Nazi Brothers Who Started Rival Shoe Companies. V128 files can stay in place.

## Open it

1. Extract the V128 Legacy source ZIP. Merge this patch's dist folder into its dist folder. Copy scripts/render-promo.mjs into its scripts folder if you want to render again.
2. Serve the merged dist folder over HTTP, for example with `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist` from the project root.
3. Open `/promo-editor.html` for V129 controls or `/review-17-23.html` for the continuous timeline. The V129 editor also runs from this patch alone when its dist folder is served.

Pick a scene, select a layer, and replace it with an image. Host and Flame also accept MP4/WebM. Set copy and counter in their own section. Save the preset to retain timing, controls and embedded replacement files. Scene cue edits retime the interval between adjacent cues. Speed and reverse affect playback. Direction reverses selected entrances and exits, including the room handoff and award exit.

## Controls and timing

Canvas coordinates are 960 × 540 with origin at top left. Default layer offsets and rotation are zero, scale/opacity/depth are 1, blur is zero, and visibility is on. Rotation is in degrees, blur and offsets are in design pixels. Global speed, travel, direction, particles, glow and exposure default to 1. The cue table and layer bindings are in dist/promo-sequence.js. The source report covers the reference timings and narration relationships.

The renderer includes a tilted media board and layered confetti, textured tower debris, a ledge wipe, replaceable inset video, perspective analytics card and highlight, masked window swaps, nested editor art, foreground clapperboards, staggered badges, a dotted path, blueprint, counter, community cards, independent clock hands, cash fan, fire transition, and a gold award exit that exposes the following text. The CTA and URL exchange while the arrows persist.

## Asset provenance and fidelity

- Room, city, London and wood are newly generated plates. City-rear is a derived patch of the generated skyline.
- Pagoda, coast and the short looping host inset are crops from the supplied 360p reference. Replace these bindings if using separately cleared or higher-resolution media.
- The opening still comes from the end of the V128 review. Cash is reused from V128's included banknote asset. Flame is a short loop derived from the existing fire atlas.
- Figure silhouette, UI, icons, clock, award and text are drawn in code. The promo panels use generic replacement copy and artwork. The window masks, tower breakup, nested views, light and fire are visual approximations.
- The clean and comparison renders are silent. Picture cut timing was used as the sync reference. No speech cue or sound effects were added.

## Render

With Node, FFmpeg and @napi-rs/canvas available, run `node scripts/render-promo.mjs path/to/review.mp4`. The default render is 640 × 360 at 24 fps for 120 seconds. The aligned comparison puts the supplied reference on the left and V129 on the right.
