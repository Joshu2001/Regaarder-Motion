## Details easy to miss

1. The lens exits before the paper fragments and fire: these are successive actions, not simultaneous random overlays.
2. The paper fragments show pale reverse faces and briefly cross the factory/face; their angular silhouettes are readable before fire occludes them.
3. The officials are more distant and softer than the near shoulder. The lamp drives paper/table highlights and facial shadows; a bloom sprite alone does not reproduce this.
4. The shoulder passage changes viewpoint while preserving the visual room language. Rotating a rear cutout would expose a flat card.
5. At610.8s the frame is nearly white because the camera is extremely close to paper. Mean image luma jumps from the dark room to about227/255; this is diagnostic evidence of the entrance, not proof of a lightning effect.
6. Newspaper curvature affects text and image together. A wavy background behind straight floating text would look disconnected.
7. The dark newspaper-to-prison interval has structural content and moving edges; darkness is intentional, not a missing scene to fill with a hold.
8. Prison hands grip specific bars and rail; layer order must allow fingers/knuckles and metal to overlap plausibly.
9. The same camp picture becomes the photo. A fresh portrait appearing during pullback would break continuity.
10. The folder's front cover physically hides the photo. Keep its upper edge and cast shadow ahead of the photo, not behind it.
11. Table tilt preserves attachment of the folder/stamp to the surface. Perspective must affect all four corners, contact shadows and thickness consistently.
12. The courtroom is revealed below the moving desk edge before the preceding scene disappears. The witness swap near654s is lateral; avoid sequential pop-in.
13. This minute relies on dim pools of light and restrained color. Additional lightning/red flashes are not evident in the interrogation/prison/courtroom beats and should not be added automatically.
