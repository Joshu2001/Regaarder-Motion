## Added findings beyond Joshua's annotations

3. The wooden tabletop and Mexico poster share the camera roll. Matching only a spinning poster would be visibly wrong.
4. Dollar denomination changes between compositions. A large $1 bill is not an upscale error to be replaced globally by $100 bills.
5. The final football passage has both an initial pullback and a later abrupt boot punch-in, followed by an occluded ring. These are distinct cues.
6. Blue-left/red-right continuity persists across restaurant, portraits, logos and tabletop. Warm light is localized to props; foreground is chiefly cool. A blanket saturation change cannot reproduce this lighting layout.
7. The newspaper tableau needs actual historical source images and legible headlines where possible. Existing project asset packs should be inspected before any new generation; their presence alone does not verify source identity or matching framing.
