## Additional details the annotations did not fully capture

1. The opening archival border is inherited from the previous sequence. Do not duplicate its entrance or create a continuity reset at20:14.
2. The film burn overlaps the cut into the TV scene. A burn that finishes before a clean cut will have the wrong rhythm.
3. There is a multi-CRT installation around the main screen, not just a single floating television. Count of peripheral sets is not locked from360p.
4. The CRT image keeps playing; its reflected image and the red stage reflection must remain registered while the camera retreats.
5. A near-black beat separates TV and dining. The Pelé celebration cut is different: a hard cut without that dark reveal.
6. Foreground banknotes have dark backs and strong depth blur. A field of uniformly sharp front-facing $100 images would be an invention here; denomination remains unresolved.
7. Profile pose changes carry story meaning: confrontation first, then separation. Reusing one inward-facing pair would lose it.
8. The central warm-white dividing slit persists through the later profiles, coffins and grave pullback. It is not interchangeable with the fine particle overlay.
9. The ending is not a space scene on available evidence. Earth/grass revealed by the retreat anchors the burial metaphor.
10. Portrait-in-coffin images, metal bands, wood material and the coffin contour must remain a connected object. Do not replace with a polished rectangle or a cartoon gravestone.

## Lighting and restoration direction for later asset creation

Honor the user guidelines: reverse-search exact frames where a suitable reverse-search facility exists, descriptive archival search next, generation only if originals cannot be recovered. Preserve the retrieved originals, label reconstructions, and upscale/restore nondestructively. Do not regenerate a historical face or logo just because a clean plate is needed.

Enhance drama by improving light separation and readable materials, not making everything bright: screen light against red stage; red handshake/green amount over a dark dining room; warm Pelé rim versus cool streaks; saturated brand walls with dark profile interiors; restrained blue/red coffin edges and a warm central slit over near-black earth.
