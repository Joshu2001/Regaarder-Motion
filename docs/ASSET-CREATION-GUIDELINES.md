# Asset creation guidelines

When analyzing the frames for the videos I provide, analyze the lighting of the provided frames and enhance them according to the following rules:

1. **Cinematic Lighting & Dramatic Quality:** Ensure the final images have cinematic quality. Adjust and improve the lighting to make them look more dramatic, visually appealing, and contextually fitting for the script—surpassing the quality of the original frames.
2. **Archival & Reference Sourcing:**
   - **Step 1:** Reverse-search the image (e.g., Google Image Search) to locate high-resolution originals.
   - **Step 2:** If reverse search fails, search using descriptive keywords.
   - **Step 3:** As a last resort, generate a matching scene from scratch.
3. **Quality Restoration:** If an original or retrieved internet image is low quality, beautify/upscale it or regenerate a high-definition version.

## Script guidance

Use the supplied script as inspiration for context, mood and asset choices whenever unsure. If it is unavailable, consult the saved script notes and mark unresolved story details rather than inventing historical facts.

## Provenance and editability

Preserve originals and alternatives. Label archival originals, reconstructions and generated illustrations separately. Record sources, modifications and animation use. Keep image sources, masks, light, smoke, distortion, movement and aging effects separately adjustable.
