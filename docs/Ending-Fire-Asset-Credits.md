# Real flame overlays for the ending

Apply dist/ over the burning-shoe fire patch. The revised shots are Rival brands, Adidas blue, Red shoeboxes and Final rivalry. The four footage files use fixed emitter positions and independently editable layer transforms. The rivalry's dark wisps are flame silhouettes derived from footage and use Multiply. The final rivalry replaces its static foreground mist with wider moving flame overlays. The slow background cloud plates are retained. All six MP4 assets can be reused independently.
