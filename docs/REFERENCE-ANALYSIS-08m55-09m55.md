## Reusable abstractions
1. Opaque structural passage: source and destination scenes coexist; a foreground surface controls visibility. Parameters: direction, coverage boundary, depth, handoff timing and texture alignment.
2. Referential typography: timed text, emphasis and a connector anchored to a subject. Parameters: glyph timing, word color, route vertices, draw length, target anchor and attachment interval.
3. Foreground type passage: the text moves toward/past the camera while a second scene remains behind. Parameters: text depth, camera distance, near clipping, lateral travel and background hold.
4. Layered inference tableau: oversized faces plus miniature architecture express conflict through scale. Parameters: per-layer depth, face orientation, eye mask, glow pulse and environmental light.
5. Inspection lens: sample coordinates are tied to the lens center, not independent animation. Parameters: lens radius, magnification, optical falloff, distortion, handle rotation, glint and path.
6. Performance-bearing media: distinguish actual actor/machine motion from camera-only animation. Keep video media replaceable and timing independent of scene movement.
