## Generation prompts

Built-in image generation was used for two assets:

1. **Product atlas.** One3-column ×2-row transparent production atlas. Top row: cyan vintage football shorts, cyan-lit black leather football boot with three ivory stripes and studs, cyan wool knee-high sock. Bottom row: magenta shorts, red-lit black leather boot with curved ivory side stripe, magenta sock. Complete separated objects within equal cells, wide transparent gutters. Photographic woven textiles, leather creases, stitching, directional product lighting and restrained rim light. No background, labels, text or flat cartoon fills. The resulting1536×1024 RGBA atlas was technically sliced into six512×512 cells, alpha preserved; alpha bounds recorded in the manifest.

2. **Aged paper perimeter.** Use the exact selected side-by-side image as an edit reference. Target the right archival photograph's shape, with the left worn photograph as the physical edge reference. Output only an isolated gray/off-white photographic-paper perimeter with large transparent central opening and transparent exterior. Frayed fibers, small nicks, uneven worn corners, subtle curl/emulsion marks; no people, photograph content, typography, metal frame or glass. The resulting RGBA texture is composited around the original photograph without replacing its pixels.

Generated products are cinematic illustrative assets; they are not verified historical product models. Logo geometry remains existing native artwork and receives editable material shading, rather than being regenerated.
